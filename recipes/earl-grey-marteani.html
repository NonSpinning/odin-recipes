Earl Grey Marteani

Description

An innovative take on a gin sour dreamt up by Audrey Saunders, sweet, foamy, and complex.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz. earl grey infused Beefeater gin
  • 1 oz. simple syrup
  • 1 oz. pasteurized egg white
  • ¾ oz. Meyer lemon juice
  • Garnish:
    • Sugar
    • lemon peel

Steps

  1. To make infused gin, steep 2g of loose-leaf earl grey in 4½ oz. gin for 12 hours.
  2. Add infused gin, syrup, egg white and lemon juice to shaker.
  3. Rim a coupe glass with sugar.
  4. Reverse dry shake, shake with ice first, filter ice out and then shake dry vigorously to foam it up.
  5. Strain into glass, garnish with a lemon peel.
