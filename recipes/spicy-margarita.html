Spicy Margarita

Description

A mildly spicy, boozy and sweet twist on a classic.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz. Jaral del Barrio mezcal
  • ½ oz. Sisi orange liqueur
  • ½ oz. Ancho Reyes chile ancho liqueur
  • 1 oz. lime juice
  • ½ oz. agave syrup
  • 2 jalapeño slices
  • Garnish:
    • Tajín
    • Salt
    • Jalapeño coin

Steps

  1. Rim an old-fashioned glass with a mix of Tajín and salt.
  2. Muddle the jalapeño slices with the lime juice and agave syrup in a shaker.
  3. Add mezcal, orange and chile ancho liquer to the shaker, shake with cracked ice.
  4. Serve in glass over big ice cube, add jalapeño coin as garnish.
