Moscow mule

edseloh, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Description

My own experimental modifications on an easily drinkable and refreshing staple.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz. Karat vodka
  • 1 oz. lemon juice
  • 1 oz. homemade rosemary and ginger syrup
  • 2 dashes Angostura aromatic bitters
  • ~ 4 oz. sparkling water
  • Garnish:
    • Mint Sprig
    • Lime Round

Steps

  1. Fill copper mug with cracked ice.
  2. Add bitters, vodka, lemon juice and syrup to mug, stir with a bar spoon.
  3. Top up with sparkling water, gently stir.
  4. Garnish with a lime round on the edge of the mug, and a mint sprig in the drink.
